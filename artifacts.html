Artifact 3 · Definition of Done

A feature is done when people can use it

Development finishing was only half of "done". A feature counted as delivered once SOPs were published, staff were trained, and they had passed the certification quiz.

BuiltOwned with vendor teams

  • User stories and acceptance criteria agreed with business owners
  • Development complete and deployed to the test environment
  • UAT run with real store scenarios
  • No open critical defects; others logged with an owner
  • Dependencies on other vendors confirmed
not done yet

ReadyOwned with store teams

  • SOP published and versioned: what, when, who, do's and don'ts
  • L&D chapter ready: why each step matters, how to do it, and what good looks like
  • Both training batches completed, with role play
  • Staff passed the certification quiz
  • Office hours and shadow sessions scheduled for go-live
Done = Built + Ready, signed off in the tracker
Used for every Phase 1 featureChecklist template

Artifact 5 · Program cadence & escalation

A predictable rhythm, and a clear path when something gets stuck

Tracker updates were tied to the meeting rhythm, and decisions went out in writing, which kept status meetings to a minimum.

Cadence
DeliveryScrum calls

Vendor teams report progress and blockers; the tracker is updated in the call.

AdoptionDaily office hours

Sales staff bring questions during the transition; recurring issues feed the SOPs.

QualityWeekly shadow sessions

Checks on how the new process is followed on the floor.

DecisionsMinutes of meetings

Sent after every decision, so no one needs a meeting to catch up.

Vendor escalation path
