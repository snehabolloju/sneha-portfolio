about me

Hi, I'm Sneha.

Program Manager who turns messy, cross-team problems into structured programs that deliver measurable results.

I have 7+ years of operations and delivery experience across Amazon, e-commerce, luxury retail and online advertising. Most recently, I led an end-to-end CRM transformation for a luxury jewellery retailer, taking it from personal diaries to a live digital platform with 100% team adoption in four weeks, an 8% conversion lift, and 15% growth in customer retention.

What I do

I take programs from a vague problem to a working result. That means gathering requirements from people who don't speak the same language, turning them into a clear plan, keeping vendors and internal teams moving together, and making sure the people using the result are ready on day one.

How I work

  • Start with the root cause. I look for the pattern behind a problem before deciding on a fix, whether that's recurring catalog defects or a sales team losing clients.
  • Run on data, not status updates. One shared tracker for delivery, blockers and readiness, visible to everyone, so problems surface early.
  • "Done" means people are ready. A feature isn't finished when it's built; it's finished when SOPs are published and the team is trained.
  • Adoption is the real goal. Most transformations struggle with people, not technology, so I design training, support and feedback into the plan from the start.
