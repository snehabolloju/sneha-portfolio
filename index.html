How a program takes shape

From requirement to release

How I took the CRM program from scattered needs to a live platform: requirements gathered across four teams, grouped into epics, written as user stories, delivered in sprints, and handed over only when people were ready.

  1. DiscoverRequirements from Sales, Inventory, Orders and Leadership, built from scratch in ~30 days
  2. EpicsPrioritised into 4 Phase 1 feature areas
  3. User storiesWritten from three angles: the user, the customer and leadership
  4. Sprints & UAT6 vendor teams, tracked on rollovers, defects and blocker age
  5. Go-liveDone = SOPs, training and quiz. 100% adoption in 4 weeks
Phase 1 story mapRepresentative stories · CRM program
EPIC 1Customer profiles & segmentation

EPIC 2Follow-ups & order tracking
Get reminders for follow-ups and upcoming buying occasions

Track my conversion and follow-ups against target
EPIC 4Leadership dashboards
View walk-in conversion by store
Monitor customer data fill rate

As a salesperson, I want to see a customer's purchase history and upcoming occasions before I greet them, so that I can personalise the conversation and build rapport.

Acceptance criteria
  • Searching by mobile number opens the customer's profile
  • Profile shows past purchases and design preferences
  • Upcoming occasions such as birthdays and anniversaries are flagged
  • Works for customers mapped from existing account records
