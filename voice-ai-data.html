Training data quality for voice AI at Amazon

Role
ML Data Associate
Timeline
Nov 2019 – Jun 2021
Scale
30-member team · English-language audio · UK marketplace primarily, supporting Australia, New Zealand and the US as needed
95%+labelling accuracy, sustained
Topconsistently among the team's top performers

The work

My team reviewed, transcribed and labelled voice instructions that fed multi-modal model training, helping Alexa understand requests more accurately.

The challenges

  • Poor audio quality, with background static making speech hard to understand
  • Labelling guidelines that changed over time
  • A wide range of regional accents, especially across the UK

How I handled them

  • Process discipline: kept the SOP close at hand for quick reference whenever something was ambiguous, and replayed difficult audio several times before finalising a transcription
  • Clarifying the rules: when guidelines were inconsistent or unclear, I worked with trainers and, when needed, other teams to agree on how to proceed, backing my points with examples from the data and sometimes proposing solutions
  • Learning from patterns: used team huddles to discuss difficult data pipelines, common error types and best practices, comparing my error patterns with team trends to keep improving
  • Building the skill myself: to understand UK regional accents better, I spent time outside work listening to British podcasts and shows, deliberately training my ear for accents from Cockney to Scottish and Yorkshire

Why it matters

This role built the foundation for my later work: a habit of finding patterns in errors and fixing their causes, which I later scaled into root-cause analysis and automation in catalog operations.
